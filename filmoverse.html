FILMOVERSE

Movie & TV Platform View Live

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Filmoverse is a movie and TV discovery platform designed and developed from scratch, with a focus on domestic cinema. The goal was to bring films, series, and related stories together in one place, making it easier for visitors to discover new titles, revisit familiar favorites, and explore content that interests them.

I created a bold, cinematic design and developed a content-driven website that brings together interactive browsing, detailed title pages, and editorial articles. The focus was on making a growing collection of content easy to explore while maintaining a consistent visual experience across different devices.

KEY FEATURES

A custom search and filtering system allows visitors to explore movies and TV shows by category and genre, with additional options for refining results. Dedicated title pages bring together key information, trailers, and cast details, while separate Top 10 sections highlight highly rated movies and series.

The website also features a dedicated blog with individual article pages and a newsletter section for visitors interested in future updates. A content management system makes it possible to add and update movies, series, and articles without manually editing the website's code.
