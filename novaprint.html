PROJECT OVERVIEW

Novaprint is a custom-designed website built from the ground up to present a wide range of 3D printing services. The project focuses on making technical services approachable, helping visitors explore what’s available, understand how the process works, and take the next step with confidence.

I handled both the design and development, creating a distinctive visual identity with bold typography, carefully selected imagery, and a consistent layout. The result is a cohesive website that balances visual impact with practical functionality, making it easy to explore on any device.

KEY FEATURES

The website organizes five 3D printing service categories into a clear, visually driven layout, helping visitors quickly find what they need. A dedicated five-step process section breaks down the journey from an initial idea to a finished product, making the service easier to understand.

Interactive elements add functionality without unnecessary complexity. An expandable FAQ provides quick answers to common questions, while a dedicated contact form gives visitors a direct way to discuss their projects. The entire experience is adapted for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
