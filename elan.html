PROJECT OVERVIEW

ELAN is a custom-designed real estate website created to showcase distinctive properties through architecture, photography, and a refined browsing experience. The goal was to give each residence the space it deserves while helping visitors explore properties, discover their details, and make inquiries with ease.

I designed and developed the website from scratch, combining spacious layouts, carefully considered typography, and subtle animations to create a cohesive visual experience. The focus was on presenting each property clearly while maintaining a consistent and intuitive experience across the entire website.

KEY FEATURES

Each property has a dedicated page featuring image galleries, detailed descriptions, key specifications, amenities, and floor plans, giving visitors a complete overview of the residence. Related properties encourage further exploration, while inquiry options provide a direct way for potential buyers to get in touch.

The website also includes a journal with individual articles covering architecture, design, and lifestyle. A content management system allows properties and articles to be added or updated without changing the website's code, making the platform easier to maintain and expand.
